# Cookie Policy

*Last Updated: July 2026*

This Cookie Policy explains how Castiva uses cookies and tracking technologies to personalize your experience.

## 1. What are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to recall login credentials, UI selections, and profile settings.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use
- **Essential Cookies**: Necessary for security, authentication, and core page operations.
- **Analytics Cookies**: Used to measure site performance, user engagement, and traffic sources.
- **AI Matcher Cookies**: Temporary caching cookies that help speed up matching suggestions.

## 3. Managing Preferences
You can disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings, though some portal features may become unavailable.
