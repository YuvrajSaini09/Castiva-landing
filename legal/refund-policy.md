# Refund Policy

*Last Updated: July 2026*

This Refund Policy outlines our guidelines for refunding premium subscription plans, booking deposits, and escrow balances.

## 1. Subscriptions
- Monthly and yearly premium plans are non-refundable once activated.
- You can cancel your subscription at any time to avoid future renewals.

## 2. Escrow Booking Releases
- Escrow funds are subject to dispute resolution if a project is cancelled or a milestone is breached.
- If the Talent fails to attend or perform, funds are refunded to the Recruiter, minus administrative processing fees.

## 3. Discrepancies
All dispute resolutions are managed by our compliance team. Decisions are final and binding.
