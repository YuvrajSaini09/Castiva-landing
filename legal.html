Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Castiva, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and share your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile Data: Name, gender, age, location, body type, and acting/casting attributes.
  • Media Content: Showreels, audio voice files, and headshots uploaded to your portfolio.
  • Usage Data: Audition scores, location preferences, and communication logs on verified chat channels.

2. How We Use Data

  • To run our AI-driven casting matchmaker.
  • To display your digital portfolio link to verified recruiters.
  • To process secure payments and escrow structures.

3. Data Protection

Your data is securely stored and encrypted. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

User Agreement

Last Updated: July 2026

This User Agreement governs the relationships, escrows, and booking structures completed through the Castiva ecosystem.

1. Booking Contracts

  • All casting agreements processed via Castiva are legally binding contracts between the Recruiter and the Talent.
  • Castiva acts solely as a matching portal and escrow mediator, not as an employer or agency representative.

2. Escrow & Fees

  • Recruiters agree to load booking funds into Castiva's secure escrow before project start.
  • Talent payment is released upon milestone approvals as specified in the agreement.

3. Disclaimers

We enforce strict KYC and verification checks, but users are advised to perform standard security precautions before physical audition meetups.

Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Refund Policy outlines our guidelines for refunding premium subscription plans, booking deposits, and escrow balances.

1. Subscriptions

  • Monthly and yearly premium plans are non-refundable once activated.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time to avoid future renewals.

2. Escrow Booking Releases

  • Escrow funds are subject to dispute resolution if a project is cancelled or a milestone is breached.
  • If the Talent fails to attend or perform, funds are refunded to the Recruiter, minus administrative processing fees.

3. Discrepancies

All dispute resolutions are managed by our compliance team. Decisions are final and binding.

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Castiva uses cookies and tracking technologies to personalize your experience.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to recall login credentials, UI selections, and profile settings.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for security, authentication, and core page operations.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to measure site performance, user engagement, and traffic sources.
  • AI Matcher Cookies: Temporary caching cookies that help speed up matching suggestions.

3. Managing Preferences

You can disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings, though some portal features may become unavailable.
